How to play

Welcome to the World Marathon Tour team!

You're on duty at the aid station. Your duty is to toss supplies to runners with matching numbers.

Two numbers match if either can be divided by the other without a remainder.

Tap a matching runner to toss your supply. Tap any supply on the table to swap it with the one in your hand.

After each successful catch, the runner's number changes:

  • If the runner's number is greater than the supply number, divide: 12 ÷ 3 = 4
  • Otherwise, multiply: 3 × 12 = 36

Go for a high score!

Serve the same runner as many times as you can. Every extra supply brings a bigger reward.

Keep your combo growing! The higher your combo, the bigger your rewards.

Ready? Let the race begin!
