# Step 08 — Tokyo spectators and seasonal overlays

This folder has 18 editable transparent SVG crowd groupings: three modes × three depths (near / mid / far) × two sides; one independent festival ambient SVG per mode. Same people, facial styles, skin/clothes and fixed positions persist across modes. Normal visitors become sakura-festival cheering crowds in Fever and fan/lantern/fireworks spectators in Inferno. The near row includes a child with family, an older spectator and a visible wheelchair visitor.

[Three-mode crowd contact](../../../previews/steps05-08/tokyo_crowd_3_modes_r02.svg).

No official festival insignia, brand marks or live-mode behavior. Review sketches; real-time scale, rail ordering, mobile legibility and event provenance require approval. Remaining cities follow Step 12 after Tokyo approval.
